How Zerodha Kite Sync Works

The extension does not require or store your broker passwords or API secret keys. It securely reads the visible holdings table on your Kite dashboard in your browser.

1

Log in to Zerodha Kite

Open kite.zerodha.com in your browser and log into your account.

2

Navigate to Holdings

Click on the Holdings tab (kite.zerodha.com/holdings) or Positions tab.

3

Manual Sync on Demand

Click ⚡ Sync Holdings in the extension popup menu. The extension strictly reads only the stock symbols from your holdings table and never runs background scripts or touches anything else on Kite.
